Halo 3 Orbital station surface maps (Microsoft Corporation)

Source: Halo Archive public Orbital bitmaps folder,
https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1u04spNJsl5EIFYkFIqFDb6aLEIO9O0C9

Original TIFFs: mc_metal_wall_plate_a_dif, mc_metal_wall_plate_a_zbump,
mc_metal_floor_a_dif, and mc_metal_floor_a_zbump. Color maps were converted
to JPEG and bump maps to PNG for Saturn Devouring's interior materials. The
source and derived maps are Halo content excluded from the project's MIT code
license. Public availability is not a permission record for redistribution.
