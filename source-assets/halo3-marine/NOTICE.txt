Halo 3 Marine source material (Microsoft Corporation)

marine.fbx: Halo Archive public Marine model folder,
https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/16ZP2Lcsap4fX9ptoMjVpum1shsQYjxRp

bitmaps/*.tif: Halo3 Models Edition, Marine texture paths,
https://www.moddb.com/games/garrys-mod-10/addons/halo3-models-edition

animations/*: original Halo 3 Marine rifle JMA-family exports from
77Mynameislol77/HaloAnimationRepository, Halo3-Xbox/data/objects/characters/marine/marine,
https://github.com/77Mynameislol77/HaloAnimationRepository

Converted for Saturn Devouring into the six-part instanced Marine renderer.
Geometry was scaled and separated by dominant skin bone; diffuse images were
resized; source animation bone rotations were sampled and retargeted to the
rigid parts. The source exports and derived files remain Halo content and are
excluded from this project's MIT code license. Public availability is not a
permission record for redistribution. See docs/ASSET-RIGHTS.md.
